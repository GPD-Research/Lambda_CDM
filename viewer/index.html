Time runs on a log scale because most of the action happens in the first million years. Right of the white line is the future, on a log scale of time after today that is squeezed further past 10¹⁹ years; playback pauses at today. Temperature uses T = T₀/a with T₀ = 2.7255 K. Inflation is drawn as 60 e-folds ending at 10⁻³² s; the physics before ~10⁻¹² s is extrapolated, not tested at those energies. Features on the sphere are not to scale, except the CMB maps, which show the real sky at its true angular scale.

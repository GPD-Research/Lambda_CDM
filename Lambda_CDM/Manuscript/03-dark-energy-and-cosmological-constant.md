# Chapter 3: Dark Energy and the Cosmological Constant

In $\Lambda\text{CDM}$, dark energy is modeled strictly as a cosmological constant ($\Lambda$). This implies a constant energy density across space and time with an equation of state parameter:

$$w = \frac{P}{\rho} = -1$$

where $P$ is isotropic pressure and $\rho$ is energy density. 

## The Acceleration Condition

For the universe's expansion to accelerate ($\ddot{a} > 0$), the active gravitational mass must be negative, which requires:

$$\rho + 3P < 0 \implies w < -\frac{1}{3}$$

With $w = -1$, dark energy satisfies this condition completely, driving exponential-like expansion dominance at late cosmic epochs.

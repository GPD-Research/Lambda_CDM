# Chapter 5: Observational Tests and Tensions

The power of $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ lies in its testability against empirical datasets housed in your project's `data/` folder.

## Key Datasets

* **CMB Power Spectra:** Measures acoustic peaks corresponding to baryonic acoustic oscillations (BAO) in the early plasma.
* **Supernova Luminosity Distance:** Relates observed magnitude to redshift via the luminosity distance equation:

$$d_L(z) = (1+z) \int_0^z \frac{c \, dz'}{H(z')}$$

## Modern Tensions

Despite its success, $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ faces critical anomalies:
1. **The Hubble Tension:** Local measurements of $H_0$ (via Cepheids/Supernovae) yield ~73 km/s/Mpc, while early-universe CMB inferences (Planck) yield ~67.4 km/s/Mpc.
2. **The $\sigma_8$ Tension:** Measures of local matter clustering amplitude often sit lower than predictions extrapolated from early universe parameters.
